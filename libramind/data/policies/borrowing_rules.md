# Borrowing Rules

- Every registered member may borrow a maximum of **3 books** at a time.
- Loan period is **14 days** from the date of issue.
- A book may be renewed **once** for an additional **7 days**, provided:
  - The book has no active reservation by another member.
  - The book is **not overdue** at the time of renewal request.
- Reference books and journals **cannot be issued**; they are for in-library use only.
- A valid **college ID card** is compulsory for all transactions.
- Borrowing is **blocked** if the member has a pending fine exceeding **Rs. 50**.
- A reserved book is held for **2 days** once it becomes available; after that the reservation is cancelled automatically.
- Students must return books before or on the due date to avoid fines.
- Books cannot be borrowed on behalf of another member.
