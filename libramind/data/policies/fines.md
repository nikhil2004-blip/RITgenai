# Fines Policy

- A fine of **Rs. 2 per book per day** is charged for every day a book is overdue after its due date.
- Fines are calculated automatically when a book is returned or when the member's account is checked.
- If the total pending fine exceeds **Rs. 50**, the member's borrowing privileges are **suspended** until the fine is paid.
- Fines must be paid at the library counter; online payment is not currently supported.
- Fines accumulate even if the book is lost or damaged.
- Fine receipts are issued upon payment.
- Fines cannot be waived except in extraordinary circumstances approved by the Head Librarian.

## Example Calculation
If a book is returned 5 days late:  
Fine = 5 days × Rs. 2 = **Rs. 10**
