# Digital Resources

## Available Digital Resources
- The library provides access to several **online databases and e-journals** including IEEE Xplore, ACM Digital Library, Springer, and NPTEL.
- Access is available on the **college network** (Wi-Fi and LAN) without any login; off-campus access requires VPN.
- E-books on the library portal can be read online; downloads are subject to publisher restrictions.

## How to Access
1. Connect to the college network or VPN.
2. Visit the library portal at the intranet URL provided by the IT department.
3. Select the required database from the digital resources section.

## Rules for Digital Resources
- Digital resources are for **personal academic use only**; bulk downloading is prohibited.
- Sharing access credentials is not allowed and may result in suspension.
- Plagiarism from digital resources is treated under the college's academic integrity policy.
- The library does not provide printing services for e-journals; members may use the computer lab printers.

## Turnitin and Reference Management
- Students can request Turnitin access through their department for plagiarism checks.
- Zotero and Mendeley are recommended for reference management; free workshops are conducted each semester.
