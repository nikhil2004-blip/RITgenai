# Code of Conduct

## General Behaviour
- Maintain **silence** in the reading hall at all times; discussions are allowed only in the group study room.
- Mobile phones must be kept on **silent mode**; calls should be taken outside the library.
- Food and beverages are **not allowed** inside the library.
- Students must keep the library clean and tidy; use the dustbins provided.

## Use of Library Property
- Library furniture, computers, and equipment must be handled with care.
- Marking, writing in, or defacing library books is strictly prohibited and treated as damage.
- Personal belongings should be kept in the designated bag-rack area near the entrance.

## Computers and Internet
- Library computers are for academic use only (research, e-resources, assignments).
- Gaming, streaming, and social media browsing are not permitted on library computers.
- Sessions are limited to **30 minutes** when other students are waiting.

## Disciplinary Action
- Violation of conduct rules may result in a warning, temporary suspension of library privileges, or escalation to the college disciplinary committee.
- Theft of library material is a serious offence and will be reported to the college administration.

## Grievances
- Complaints or suggestions can be submitted in the library suggestion box or emailed to the Head Librarian.
