## Membership
- Library membership is open to all currently enrolled students and faculty of the college.
- Membership is activated automatically upon admission; no separate registration is required.
- Members must carry their **college ID card** for all library transactions.
- Membership remains valid as long as the student is enrolled.
- Graduating students must return all books and clear all dues before their final clearance.
- Lost ID cards must be reported to the administration office before library access can be restored.
