# Lost or Damaged Books Policy

## Lost Books
- If a borrowed book is lost, the member must report it immediately to the library.
- The member is charged: **cost of the book + Rs. 100 processing fee**.
- The book cost is determined by the last purchase price on record or current market price, whichever is higher.
- The loan is closed only after full payment is made.
- Borrowing privileges remain suspended until payment is complete.

## Damaged Books
- Minor damage (torn pages, minor stains) will be assessed and charged accordingly (minimum Rs. 50).
- Severe damage (broken spine, water damage, missing pages) is treated the same as a lost book.
- The librarian will assess damage at the time of return.

## General
- Members are responsible for the books they borrow from the moment of issue until return.
- Always check the condition of a book before borrowing and report any existing damage to avoid liability.
